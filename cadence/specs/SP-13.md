# C-13: Forgotten Clock-Out Auto-Close & Attendance Queries -- Spec

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A configurable daily cutoff exists (e.g. env/config value) that determines when auto-close runs.
- [ ] A scheduled job closes every `time_entries` row with `clock_out IS NULL` past the cutoff, setting `clock_out` to the cutoff time and `auto_closed = true`.
- [ ] Rows closed by the job are distinguishable from normally-closed rows via `auto_closed`.
- [ ] The job leaves entries that are still legitimately open and not yet past the cutoff untouched.
- [ ] An endpoint/query scope allows filtering `time_entries` by `user_id`, `role`, and date range, returning `auto_closed` status per row.
- [ ] Job behavior (closes only past-cutoff open entries, correct `auto_closed` flagging) is covered by tests.

## Out of scope
Any dashboard or reporting UI over this data -- belongs to the later Owner analytics epic. Depends on `time_entries` rows produced by [[US-11]] and [[US-12]].
