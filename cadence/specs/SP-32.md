# C-32: Cashier & Take-Orders UI Audit & Fixes -- Spec

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Cashier (Checkout) and Take-Orders (OrderTaking) screens audited
      against `ui-ux-pro-max` guidelines: accessibility, touch target
      sizing, async action feedback/loading states, spacing/typography
      consistency, Ant Design token usage.
- [ ] All concrete findings from the audit are fixed, or explicitly logged
      in this item's notes as an out-of-scope follow-up with a stated
      reason.
- [ ] The full frontend test suite passes with no regressions on both
      screens.

## Out of scope
The app shell itself ([[US-29]]) and any other page's audit.

See [[DS-32]] for rationale and trade-offs.
