# C-33: Kitchen UI Audit & Fixes -- Spec

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `KitchenDisplay` and `KitchenClockPad` audited against `ui-ux-pro-max`
      guidelines: accessibility, spacing/typography consistency, Ant
      Design token usage, visual polish -- without regressing the existing
      large-touch-target / at-a-distance-readability design intent.
- [ ] All concrete findings from the audit are fixed, or explicitly logged
      in this item's notes as an out-of-scope follow-up with a stated
      reason.
- [ ] The full frontend test suite passes with no regressions on the
      Kitchen screen.

## Out of scope
The app shell itself ([[US-29]]) and any other page's audit.

See [[DS-33]] for rationale and trade-offs.
