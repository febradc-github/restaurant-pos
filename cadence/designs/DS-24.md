# C-24: Sales & Best-Seller Analytics API -- Design

## Parent
Part of [[EP-23]] -- see [[DS-23]] for the umbrella rationale.

## Problem
No sales-aggregation or best/worst-seller ranking exists anywhere in the backend. The Owner has no way to see revenue trends or which menu items are winning/losing.

## Approach
Add local query scopes to `Order` mirroring `TimeEntry`'s chainable pattern (`forUser`/`forRole`/`clockedInBetween`), e.g. `scopePaid` and `scopePaidBetween`, filtered to `OrderStatus::Paid` and dated by `paid_at` (confirmed to exist and be set at checkout by `PaymentConfirmationService`). Add an Owner-gated endpoint returning a sales-over-time series (revenue grouped by day within a date range) and a best/worst-seller endpoint returning menu items ranked by both quantity sold and revenue over a date range, computed by joining `OrderItem` -> `Order` (filtered Paid) -> `MenuItem`.

## Acceptance criteria
- Owner-gated endpoint returns revenue grouped by day for Paid orders within a given date range, dated by `paid_at`.
- Owner-gated endpoint returns menu items ranked by quantity sold and by revenue (both metrics present per item) for Paid orders within a given date range.
- Cancelled, Pending, and Ready orders are excluded from both endpoints -- only `Paid` orders contribute.
- Query logic uses `Order` local scopes (chainable, mirroring `TimeEntry`'s pattern) rather than one-off raw SQL.
- Both endpoints are covered by tests using real mutation/query checks (seeded orders of varying status, confirming exclusion of non-Paid orders and correct ranking/grouping).
