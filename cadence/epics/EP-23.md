# C-23: Owner Analytics Dashboard

Gives the Owner data-driven visibility into the restaurant's performance: a sales trend chart and best/worst-selling menu items (quantity and revenue) computed from Paid orders, staff attendance/hours from the existing time_entries API, and a live inventory restock view with system-suggested per-ingredient thresholds derived from usage history. Surfaces as a new "Analytics" section in OwnerPage. Closes out the original "digital transformation" goal.
