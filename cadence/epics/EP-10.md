# C-10: Attendance & Extended RBAC

Extends the shipped [[EP-1]] auth system so Server (waiter) gains real login and Cashier's existing login/logout is retrofitted to record attendance, giving every authenticated role a time-in/time-out trail via a new `time_entries` model. Kitchen staff, who share one device and must keep their no-login order display, instead clock in/out through a PIN-pad overlay on that same screen. Foundational for the not-yet-refined Owner employee-management and analytics-dashboard epics from the same digital-transformation initiative.
