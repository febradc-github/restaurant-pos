# C-13: Forgotten Clock-Out Auto-Close & Attendance Queries

Adds a scheduled job that auto-closes any `time_entries` row left open past a configurable daily cutoff (flagged `auto_closed`), plus a query surface for filtering attendance by user, role, and date range -- the data foundation the future Owner analytics dashboard will read from.
