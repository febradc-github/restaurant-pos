# C-12: Kitchen PIN Clock-In/Out

Gives Kitchen staff real `UserRole::Kitchen` accounts identified by a unique 6-digit PIN, and a lightweight PIN endpoint (no Sanctum session) that toggles clock-in/clock-out. A PIN-pad overlay on the Kitchen display lets staff punch in/out without gating or interrupting the shared, no-login order view.
